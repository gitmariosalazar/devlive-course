"Time tracking isn't about you; it's a performance mirror for your manager."
Tracking Time is fundamental practice in agile methodologies like scrum, where the goal is to monitor both the time spent on tasks and the remaining time. This practice allows teams to better manage their progress within a Sprint, aligning with the established goals. Additionally, it helps improve the accuracy of estimates and provides valuable feedback on task execution.
The objective of Tracking Time is not only to measure elapsed time but also to facilitate informed decision-making for future projects. By tracking work velocity and analyzing estimates versus actual time spent, teams can optimize their planning processes, identity potential obstacles, and adjust they strategies to improve efficiency in future interactions.
