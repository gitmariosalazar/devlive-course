### Spring Planning 
Here, the team will review the project's product backlog and narrow down what they'd like to prioritize for the next sprint.
Each task will also be assigned a sprint team member. This type of scrum meeting aims to ensure everyone involved is on the same page before the works begins.
At the end of the planning meeting, every scrum team member must by clear on what can be delivered in the sprint and how it'll be accomplished.
### Backlog Refinement
Are also referred to as backlog grooming meetings, as they are used to discuss projects or assignments within the backlog.
The goal of backlog refinement is to keep this running list as accurate and as up-to-date as possible, along with as many details as the team can get their hands on.
### Daily Standup
This purpose of this meeting is to keep the team updated on what you are working on. Usually, an excellent daily standup or "DSU" should take less than 15 minutes, and each person on the team needs to give an update on their work.
- **Questions**
	- What did I accomplish since the last daily scrum?
	- What do I plan to work an by the next daily scrum?
	- What are the obstacles or impediments that are preventing me from making progress?
By answering these questions, we assist the scrum master in getting a sense of everyone's progress and how likely the team will achieve the various milestones they have throughout the project timeline. 
### Sprint Review
The scrum team comes together to show off what was accomplished and will usually demo its features and functionality. In addition to the team, attendees can include upper management, customers, or developers working on other products.
The final product will also be compared against the original goals of the sprint to see if they match up.
### Sprint Retrospective 
A sprint retrospective is a discussion or conversation after the sprint has closed. The team will come together to review what happened during the sprint, including: 
- What worked well?
- What didn't go as planned?
- What can be improved for the next sprint?
Here, the whole team is an attendance around 1 hour. There's always room for improvement., and how to improve will be discussed in a retrospective.
