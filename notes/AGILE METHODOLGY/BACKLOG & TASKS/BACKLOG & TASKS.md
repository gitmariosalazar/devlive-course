## Product Log

The product backlog on a project includes new features, changes to existing features, defect needing repair, and technical improvements. The product owner, the SCRUM team and stockholders, is ultimately responsible for determining and managing the sequence of this work and communicating it in the form of a prioritized list known as the product backlog.

### Epic 

An epic is a large piece of work or module looking at in from the development side that can be broken down into smaller user stories.

### User Stories

User stories are a convenient format for expressing the desired business value for many product backlog items, especially features. User stories are crafted in a way that makes them understandable to both business people and technical people. In addition, they are structurally simple and provide and excellent placeholder for a conversation.

Three C: According to Ron, the three C's of a user story are card, conversation, and confirmation.

### Message Convention

A standard template format for writing user stories is to specify a class of users (the user role), what that class of users want to achieve the goal (the benefit).

### Tasks

Task are the smallest unit used in the scrum to track work. One team member should complete a task, though the group may choose to pair up doing the work. Typically, each user story will have multiple associated tasks.
