### Stake Holders

These are people who are involved in the project or someone who is actively involved in the project.
### Product Manager

this person has multiple names: like project manager, scrum master, and others. "Product Manager is the person that is the bridge between the development team and the product owner, they are responsible for the process of the product and communicate the most efficiently possible what the team needs to develop"
### Product Owner

This is the person or entity owner of the product, they know what has to be, they are the person who has the last word on development, but they also receive suggestions from the managers or architects when the decision-making.
### UX/UI Team

These people are in charge of the design and prototypes of the final project. Still, it seems pretty cool, but is not necessarily a good design solution if the usability could be better.
UX: User Experience is the contra-part of the UI, and both are required to have the perfect product.
### Development Team

We are responsible for making the design a reality. Understand and execute the idea of the best possible thought software. We can work in different areas of the software development process.
### Frontend 

They are responsible for making the visual product and are close to the designers to receive and give feedback about the progress. So, there are different ways for a user to access the product's content. For example: web sites. (Mobile Developers, Web Developers: both perform in different programming languages or frameworks).
### Backend

The developers responsible for the business logic, often identified by the term API, are in charge of building the part of the software that users don't see but is essential for the company's operations. They handle the security and integrity of the data by interacting directly with the database, which is critical for any business's success. Data, when analyzed, provides crucial insights that drive progress into new revolutionary industries

### Full - Stack

A Full-Stack developer is the person who works on both the frontend and backend. It is the combination of both worlds (Backend and Frontend).

Quality Assurance Team

This team is responsible for testing how the product performs how it's supposed to.
### DevOps

They are in charge of automating the process for the release of the different features and environments that a product lives on. Also, help set up other services we can use, like Notification Services, Email services, File Services, and so...
### Customers 

Customers are a crucial part of the development flow, as they provide us with direct or indirect information about our products. We often choose some customers to test the app ang gather feedback. However, doing this process manually can be time-consuming, which is why customer behavior tracking is done when creating products. By collecting indirect data from users, we can analyze it and obtain valuable, real information.
